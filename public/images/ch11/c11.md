## **Chapter 11 – Filtered Love Begins (May 2025)**

2025, May month.

party, dinner purpose tho.

but Madhu **feelings still restrained**.
He has feelings… kani **marriage, future thoughts, serious plans… not yet**.

**The Marriage Question**

Some days tarvata, Priyanka **direct ga adigindi**:
**“Bava… shall we marry?”**

* **Different sub-caste**
* **Financial gap** (Priyanka family richer than Madhu)
* **Father approval issue**

Madhu **explained softly**:
**“Bujji… idhi easy kaadu…
Mana caste difference, family differences…
Future lo complications vachayi…”**

Priyanka **understood, smiled softly**, explained:
**“Neeku growth kavali…
Salary, position improve chesuko…
Ilaage, careful planning… taruvata easy ga marry chesukovachu…
Caste issue minor ga ignore cheyyachu…
Patience tho step by step cheyyali…
Bava… actual love start chestham…
Kaani filtered love…
Any problem like father rejection unte…
We will leave, no pressure…
Neither devadas, neither dull…
Always happy, normal life maintain…
Step by step… careful ga…”**

Madhu **heart fully accept chesadu**…
soft filtered love start ayyindi.
This is **real love**, careful, mature, but emotional…
Mutual understanding, respect, and patience lo strong foundation build ayyindi.
