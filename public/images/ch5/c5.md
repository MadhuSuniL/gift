## **Chapter 5 – The Rubik’s Cube Moment (2018)**

2018 year.
Madhu ki appudu **18–19 years** untayi.
Priyanka ki around **15–16 years**.

Ippatiki iddaru chinna pillalu kaadhu.
Konni feelings ardham ayyei… konni shyness, attraction…
mari relatives mundu konchem careful ga behave cheyyalani telisindi.

Aa samvatsaram Priyanka intlo small celebration plan chesaru.
Tana nanna first floor kattadam complete ayyindi kabatti,
okka chinna party arrange chesaru.
Madhu family kuda invite ayyindi.

Madhu akkada ki vellaga atmosphere konchem different ga anipinchindi.
Iddaru ippudu normal ga mataadey stage ki ochchesaru,
shyness thaggipotunnadi…
kaani lopala okka light blush, okka silent spark iddarlo untunde.
<BREAK>
Kids pakkaki kadili kadili aadutunte,
elders pakkana matladutunte,
akkada jarigina **small moment** matram both heart lo deep ga undipoyindi...

**Rubik’s Cube moment.**

Madhu tana **3×3 Rubik’s Cube** tiskoni vachadu.
A recent ga solve chesi nerchukunna hobby.

Priyanka first time adhi solve chesthunna Madhu ni chusi
okka surprise + admiration look ichindi.

“**Nuvvu idi solve chesthava?**” ani wide eyes tho adigindi.

Madhu soft ga navvadu:
“**Haa… ippudu niku kuda nerpista.**”

Chaalu… aa oka sentence tho atmosphere change aipoyindi.
<BREAK>
Priyanka tana chotu nunchi move ayi,
Madhu daggara **very close** ga kurchundi.
A first closeness…
but natural ga, reason Cube kabatti.

Madhu step by step cheppadam start chesadu—
colors ela match cheyyali,
rotations ela cheyyali,
patterns ela gurthupettukovali.

Teaching lopala iddari hands konni sarlu touch ayayi…
empty ga, accidental ga…
kaani feelings lo intha empty kaaledu.

Relatives andariki iddaru **“Rubik’s Cube ne chustunnaru”** anipinchadam vallaa,
no teasing… no drama.
Madhu ki Priyanka tho inka ekkuva time dorikindi.
Priyanka genuine ga interest chupinchindi.
Madhu pakkanunchi levakunda solve cheyadaniki try chesthunde,
Madhu ki adi nachichindi.
<BREAK>
Idi iddari life lo **first long time together**.
Laughs, teasing, small fights…
Madhu chinnaga tease chesthe
Priyanka “Peh!” ani light ga hit chesthundi.

Aa chemistry slow gaa… silent gaa…
heart lo settle ayipotundi rendu vaallaki.

Event ayyaka, Priyanka already konni steps solve chesthundani proud ga andariki chupinchindi.
Madhu pakkana nunchi soft smile tho chusthune unnadu.
A smile… Priyanka kosame.

Aa party ayipoyindi...
kani **Rubik’s Cube moment** matram iddari life lo permanent ayipoyindi.
