## **Chapter 15 – The Final Push (October 2025)**

**2025 October 7th.**

Chat silent ayyi **two days** complete ayyai.

Madhu (Kanna) phone chudatam kuda manesadu.
Guilt, pain, self-hate tho silent ga undipoyadu.



**After Two Days – Priyanka Messages Again**

Suddenly…
Notification.

**Message from Bujji.**

Madhu heart skip ayindi.

**Priyanka:**

> *“Kanna…”*
> *“Sorry.”*
> *“It’s okay.”*
> *“Past lo nuvvu em chesina parledhu.”*
> *“Nuvvu ippudu naatho unnaavu kada.”*
> *“Adhi chaalu.”*
> *“Past is past.”*
> *“Nannu vadileyaku.”*
> *“Please marry me.”*
> *“Nenu ninnu vadilalenu.”*
> *“Nuvve naa life.”*

Ee messages chadivina ventane…
**Madhu breaks down again.**

Tears…
Hands shaking…
Mind blank.

*“Ilaanti truth telisina taruvata kuda…”*
*“Still she loves me?”*

<BREAK>

**Madhu’s Fear Turns Cruel**

Madhu mind lo okate thought:

> *“She will never leave me unless I break her completely.”*

So…
He decides to become **cruel on purpose**.

<BREAK>

**Madhu Replies – Rudely (On Purpose)**

**Madhu:**

> *“Bujji… past is past ani nuvvu antunnav.”*
> *“Ante enti artham ?”*
> *“Ante nuvvu kuda past lo chaala mandi tho tirigav kada.”*
> *“Anduke le past is past antunnav.”*
> *“Naku doubts vastunnayi.”*

Priyanka stunned.

**Priyanka:**

> *“What are you saying?”*
> *“Nenu alanti ammayini kaadu.”*
> *“Nuvvu nannu ela doubt chestunnav?”*

**Madhu (cold & repeated):**

> *“Nenu doubts feel avtunna.”*
> *“Nuvvu kuda bad person ey.”*
> *“Naku clarity ledu.”*



**Priyanka Breaks Completely**

Eyes full tears.
Hands shaking while typing.

**Priyanka:**

> *“Nenu ninnu true ga love chesanu.”*
> *“Nuvvu nannu ala matladutunnav?”*
> *“I trusted you.”*

But Madhu listens to nothing.

He keeps repeating the same lines.
No explanation.
No softness.

<BREAK>

**The Final Decision**

Finally…
Priyanka heart **completely breaks**.

She types the last message.

**Priyanka:**

> *“Enough.”*
> *“I can’t do this anymore.”*
> *“Let’s stop.”*

Madhu replies immediately.

**Madhu:**

> *“Okay.”*
> *“Let’s break up.”*



**End of Everything**

Chat ends.
No blocks.
No last seen drama.
Just **silence**.

Two people who loved deeply…
Destroyed each other to protect each other.

* **Priyanka** – broken, confused, empty
* **Madhu** – alive, but dead inside



## **End of Chapter 15**
