## **Chapter 8 – Silent Tears (2020)**

2020 year.
Madhu ki **20 years**, Priyanka ki **17 years**.
Iddaru ippudu **love ni, feelings ni, maturity ni, ardham chesukunnaru**.
Childhood innocence kadhu… ippudu subtle emotions, unspoken thoughts, silent glances.

Aa samvatsaram, Madhu **tana sister marriage preparation lo busy**.
Planning, arrangements, money work…
chala hectic schedule.
<BREAK>
Priyanka initially **marriage ki vachindi kaadu**.
Aame matrame last main event ki vellindi.
Total ga 3–4 hours matrame akkada undi.

Madhu busy ga andariki instructions ivvadam, managing, running around…
Aame silent ga corner lo undi.

Suddenly… Madhu **aame ni chusi**…
thanu edusthundi ani gamaninchaadu..

Adi **Madhu kosam kaadu…**
Adi ardham avvaledu.
Asal priyanka madhu ni choodaledu
<BREAK>
**“Ela… why she is crying? Emi ayindi?”**
Thanaki ardham kaaledu…
Aame face lo chinna princess-like sadness, distant ga feel ayi…
Madhu **approach cheyyalani try chesadu**.
Kaani ippudu…
he realized: **she is like a princess… and I am not suitable… cannot reach… cannot touch that world.**

So Madhu just stood, **silent observer**.
He watched her…
but **did not move**,
did not question,
did not interfere.
<BREAK>
Aame **unknowingly crying… not because of Madhu**,
kaani Madhu heart lo subtle tension, worry, curiosity remain ayi.

After some time…
aame **disappeared from the event** quietly.

Madhu returned to his work…
busy managing sister’s marriage…
but heart lo **oka chinna soft wound** undi.

Ilaage Chapter 8 end ayiindi—
**silent glances, unspoken feelings, distance, and soft heartbreak**.
