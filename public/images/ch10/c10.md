## **Chapter 10 – Reunion and Rekindled Feelings (2024)**

2023, mid-year.

Some months later, **job start ayina tarvata**,
Madhu **first time message chesadu**.
Priyanka **same reply istundi**:
**“Please, message cheyyaku…”**
Madhu **silent ga accept chesadu**… nothing more.

<BREAK>

**Slow Interest Rekindled**

2024, mid-year, 
Madhu, after a long time, **Priyanka village ki vachadu**.
Years pass ayyi, childhood and teen memories mind lo…
heart lo still soft feelings undi.
<BREAK>
Priyanka distance lo undi… but **interest thodu grow ayyindi**.
Lively ga Madhu ni chusi, heart lo curiosity… subtle emotions slowly surface.

Event complete ayyaka, Madhu **house ki return ayyadu**,
phone check chesadu…
2 days tarvata, **first message Priyanka nundi**:

**“Hlo”**
Normal, light, casual chat…
no deep late-night chatting, no pressure…
just soft playful **bond rekindle**.

Madhu **shock ayyadu**…
Priyanka normal ga, playful ga… chatting start chesindi.
Small words… soft laughs…
heart connection **slowly rekindle**.

<BREAK>

**Increasing Bond**

Some months, few more visits…
Madhu repeated Priyanka village visits…
Priyanka gradually **more interest show chesindi**,
lively ga, subtly… softly…
she started chatting more frequently, light playful tone…
soft teasing, smiles in texts…

Ilaage, 2024 lo **Priyanka – Madhu connection gradually rekindle ayi**,
chatting **increase ayi**,
bond strong but soft, natural, effortless.
