## **Chapter 1 — The First Feeling (Joint Wheel Scene)**

2010 lo, chinna village lo jarige **Gangamma Thirunala event** tho ee story start ayyindi.
Chala rojula taruvatha event jarugutondani, village motham lighting, sound, colours tho full lively ga undi.
Madhu family kuda vachcharu — mainly because idi tana **ammamma gaari village**.

Akkada 10 years unna **Madhu**, chinnodu, innocent ga, relatives tho nadusthu event ni enjoy chestunnadu.

Ala crowd lo nadusthunnappudu…
Suddenly Madhu steps stop ayyayi.
<BREAK>
Because he saw **her**.

**Joint wheel** lo, unna small boxes lo oka box meeda oka chinna ammayi kurchoni undi — **Priyanka**.
6–7 years, cute ribbons, bright eyes, and that small chinnari smile.

Madhu ki aame evaro teliyadu.
motta modatisaari thanu aa ammayi sorry sorry aa chinnari ni choosaadu.
<BREAK>Kani aa one second lo tana heart lo oka **first unknown feeling** puttindi.
Oka chinna prema spark laga…
Oka soft excitement chest lo kalisindi.
Madhu aa chinnaari ni choodadam first time anedi entha njm oo,
thana heart lo vachina aa new feeling kooda first time annadi anthey njm.

Priyanka Madhu ni gamaninchatledu.
Kani Madhu… aame joint wheel box lo kurchoni unde aa image ni…
Life lo first time *feel* ayyadu.

That Gangamma Thirunala event madhya…
Sounds, lights, people — anni blur ayipoyayi.
Priyanka matrame tana world lo clear ga undipoyindi.

Ala…
Rendu chinnari jeevithallo oka **silent beginning** start ayyindi.
