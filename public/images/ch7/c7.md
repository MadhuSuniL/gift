## **Chapter 7 – The Second Priyanka (2018–2019)**

*Sometimes destiny shows the same name twice… just to remind you what you truly want.*

Intermediate 2nd year.
Madhu life lo oka new phase —
classes, exams, friends, tuition…
and silent ga heart lo inka **original Priyanka** memories undeyi.

But oka roju…
class lo roll number call chesthunnaru.

**“Priyanka…”**

A name vintane
Madhu heart okka second beat miss ayindi.
Instant ga aa name lo
**childhood Priyanka** reflection kanipinchindi.
<BREAK>
Aame kaadu…
kotha ammayi.
Classmate.
Chala calm, simple, decent girl.

Kaani **that name**…
that name alone
Madhu lo malli soft feeling ni start chesindi.



**He didn’t love the girl… he loved the name**

Madhu himself ardam kaledu initially.
Why is he feeling something?
Is it attraction?
Is it real love?
Or is it just the *name* haunting him?
<BREAK>
Truth simple:
**Madhu wants Priyanka…
but he knows he can never marry the real Priyanka.
So his heart started loving the name itself.**

Ilaa oka innocent emotional confusion lo
Madhu slow ga
**second Priyanka** meeda feeling develop chesadu.



**Everything matched… except the main thing**

Oka point lo, Madhu chala explore chesadu:

* Aame **Thirumandarallu**
* Same caste
* Same family background
* Good behaviour
* Good heart

Literally **perfect match** ani anipinchindi.
Fate telling him:

**“This Priyanka is allowed…
…maybe this is your destiny.”**

Madhu heart lo confidence vacchindi.
Ippudu love cheppalani decide chesadu.

<BREAK>

**The truth hits softly, not harshly**

Oka gentle moment lo,
Madhu tana feelings anni genuine ga share chesadu.

Aame chinna silent smile tho reply ichindi:

**“Madhu…
Nenu already okarina love lo unna…
Nenu ninnu respect chesthanu…
manam friends ga continue avvachu.”**

Adhi harsh ga kaadu,
soft, respectful, kind rejection.

A moment lo Madhu heart lo
chinna crack ayyindi…
kani break kaaledu.

Reason?

Because Madhu knew
**this was not real love…
this was the shadow of another Priyanka.**

<BREAK>

**They didn’t become lovers… but became very good friends**

From that day
love lekunda
okka pure friendship start ayyindi.

Laughing,
helping in studies,
group chats…
anni normal ayyayi.

Madhu respect chesadu.
Aame honesty appreciate chesadu.

Years later,
aame tana love tho **marriage** ayyindi.
Madhu felt happy for her,
because she deserved that happiness.



**A small chapter closed…

but the real story still waiting.**

Ee chapter Madhu ki oka lesson ichindi:

**“Real love doesn’t get replaced.
Even with the same name.”**

And deep inside…
the *childhood Priyanka* feeling —
the true seed planted in 2010 —
was still quietly alive.
