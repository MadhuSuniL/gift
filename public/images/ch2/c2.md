## **Chapter 2 — The Word That Changed Everything**

Gangamma Thirunala event ayyaka, Madhu family tana **ammamma gaari illu** ki tirigi vachcharu.
Evening breeze, chinna village sounds, andharkanna deep ga Madhu mind lo okkate repeating…

**“Aame evaru? Malli eppudu chooshaanu…”**

Cot mida kurchoni, legs dangle chesthu, 10 years unna chinnodu laaga kaadu…
Oka deep thought lo undipoyadu.
Aa joint wheel lo chusina Priyanka image — rounds lo revolve avuthune undi.

Kaani…
Problem okkate.
Village anta chusinappudu kuda aame dorakaledu.

Madhu:
**“Evaru aa ammayi… epudu mallli kanipistundi…”**
Ani inside lo muttering.
<BREAK>
Appude…
House door daggara okkasari chinna sound.

Priyanka!

Aame tana peddamma illu ki means Madhu ammamma illu ki, happily running ga ochindi.
Because **Priyanka house, Madhu ammamma gari house pakkapakkane**.

Madhu freeze ayipoyadu.
Heart lo aa same spark.
Chinnari Priyanka matram emi gamaninchakunda inside ki vachchi, something take chesi velipoyindi.

Kaani Madhu…
Eyes full ga aame mida ne.
Oka moment lo tana world again silent.
<BREAK>
Aame vellipoyina tharvatha, Madhu immediate ga tana mother ni adigadu:

**“Amma… aa ammayi evaru?”**

Mother smile tho reply ichindi:

**“Aame… nee cousin Priyanka. Mana relatives ee vallu.”**

Madhu ears lo okkate echo ayyindi —

**Cousin.**

A word he never heard properly.
A word he didn’t understand.

So he asked again, innocently:

**“Amma… cousin ante enti?”**

Mother laughing ga, simple ga cheppindi:

**“Ante… nuvvu pelli chesukoovadaniki paniki vachche varusa nanna...”**

A sentence…
Madhu heart lo bullet laga padindi.

Tana feeling Priyanka gurinchi kadu…
**Aa cousin ane word gurinchi.**
Adhi new, confusing, scary, unknown emotion.

At 10 years old…
Love ante teliyadu.
Feeling ante teliyadu.
Kaani aa roju, aa word tho life lo **first pain**, **first confusion**, **first spark** start ayyayi.

The story didn’t begin with love…
It began with **that one word**.

**Cousin.**
