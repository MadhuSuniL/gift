## **Chapter 3 — First Words, First Shyness** *(Corrected)*

2012.

Exactly rendu years taruvāta… the same village lo,
**Priyanka peddamma kuthuri (akka) marriage** jarugutondi.
And the groom…
**Madhu side nundi vadina**.

One single marriage…
But two small hearts ki **second meeting**.

Appatiki Priyanka ki **9–10 years**,
Madhu ki **12–13 years**.
Innocence inkā undi…
But understanding konchem perigindi.

Ippudu rendu well aware —
**“We are cousins.”**

A knowledge valla…
Matladadam ante okka strange shyness.
<BREAK>
Because marriage lo relatives ante teasing masters:

*“Ayyo ayyo… bava maradallu kalisi matladutunnaru… eppudu pelli?”*

Ala evaraina joke chesthe,
Priyanka cheeks full red,
Madhu eyes down…
Iddaru silent ga side ki vellipotharu.

Kani…
Shyness unna, conversation avoid cheyyaleni moments kuda vachchai.
<BREAK>
Kitchen lo water thechukodaniki vellina Priyanka, plate tiskodaniki vachhina Madhu…
Ala okkasari face-to-face.

Priyanka little smile…
Madhu small laugh…
Their first natural **“Hi…”**.

Aame okkasari by mistake:

**“Anna… water ivvava?”**

Madhu immediate ga shock:

Priyanka shy ga:

**“Sorry… Madhu…”**

Aa word —
Madhu.
Direct, simple, soft.
Madhu inside lo deep echo ayindi.

Madhu kuda, aame ni **Priyanka** ane pilichevaadu.
No “cousin words”.
No “bava” or “mardal”.
Just names.
Just comfort.

Marriage full busy, loud, crowded…
Kani rendu chinna hearts madhya, oka silent bond develop ayyindi.

Ala…
**Chinna smiles, accidental talks, and shared shyness** tho
Chapter 3 quietly ended —
Feelings little deeper…
Understanding little stronger.
