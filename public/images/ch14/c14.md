## **Chapter 14 – The Silent Sacrifice (Turning Point)**

**Bujji (Priyanka)** and **Kanna (Madhu)** madhya love **peak stage** ki reach ayindi.

Ippudu:

* Names kaadu… **Bujji & Kanna**
* Full love, full care
* Future dreams
* Daily chatting
* Emojis, feelings, everything

They are **deeply in love** ❤️



**Two Sides of Madhu**

Priyanka Madhu tho unnapudu:

* Madhu baaga navvutadu
* Jokes vestadu
* Love express chestadu
* Full happy ga untadu
<BREAK>
**But…**

Priyanka lenappudu,
Madhu okkadiga unnapudu,
his mind slowly starts hurting him.

He thinks:

* *“Am I really correct for her?”*
* *“She comes from a very rich family…”*
* *“Nenu tanaki future lo happiness ivvagalana?”*

Salary gurinchi:

* *“Ippudu 70k earn chestunna”*
* *“Future lo 1L or 1.5L vachina…”*
* *“Still, will she be happy?”*

Family difference gurinchi fear:

* Rich family
* Lifestyle difference



**A Painful Decision**

Chaala deep pain tho,
Madhu oka decision tiskuntadu.

> **“Love ante compulsory marriage kaadu…”**
> **“Love ante tana happiness…”**

He convinces himself:

* *“Her father will find a perfect man for her”*
* *“She will be more happy without me”*
* *“Naa tho unte maybe initial ga happy…”*
* *“Later struggles untayi emo”*

So finally,
**Madhu decides to sacrifice his love.**

<BREAK>

**Fake Truth – Chat Lo Start**

Madhu decide ayyadu:
👉 **She should leave me. Only when she hate me.**

So chat lo slowly start chestadu.

**Madhu:**

> *“Bujji… naku konni bad habits unnayi.”*
> *“I drink… I smoke.”*
> *“Earlier nenu neeki cheppaledu.”*

**Priyanka shocked… but replies immediately:**

**Priyanka:**

> *“Enduku abaddam cheppav?”*
> *“It’s okay kannaaa.”*
> *“Ee reason kosam nannu vadilestava?”*
> *“Shut up.”*
> *“Ippatinunchi thagaku anthey kada.”*

**Madhu:**

> *“Nenu vatini vadalalenu.”*
> *“I am addicted.”*

**Priyanka (still loving):**

> *“Hmmm…”*
> *“Health paadavuthundi kannaa…”*
> *“Okay… Sare le chinna chinna ga maanedduvule..”*
> *“But don’t leave me for this.”*



**The Cruel Lie (Chat Turning Point)**

Madhu heart inka break avtundi.
*“Bad habits tho kuda thanu accept chestundi…”*

So he types the **most dangerous lie**.

**Madhu:**

> *“Naku earlier physical relationships kooda unnayi.”*
> *“Intermediate time lo.”*
> *“Nenu nee life disturb cheyyalenu.”*
> *“You deserve better.”*

**Priyanka:**

> *“Kannaa Apaddaalu cheppaku.”*
> *“Nenu ninnu blindly trust chesanu.”*
> *“Nuvvu alanti vaadivi kaadu.”*

**Madhu:**

> *“Nenu alantivaadine...”*

ala madhu serious avuthaadu

Ee messages chadivina taruvata,
**Priyanka completely shocked.**

Konni seconds typing indicator…
Tarvata okate message.

**Priyanka:**

> *“You cheated me.”*
> *“Nenu ninnu blindly trust chesanu.”*

Tarvata…
**She stops replying.**
No messages.
No typing.
Just silence.

<BREAK>

**Two Hearts, Same Pain**

Chat silent ayyaka:

* **Madhu breaks completely**

  * Guilt
  * Crying
  * Self-hatred
  * Love sacrifice pain

* **Priyanka also cries alone**

  * Shock
  * Betrayal
  * Broken trust
  * Still loving him

Both are crying.
Both are loving.
But **both are broken.**


## **End of Chapter 14**

💔 Love without hate
💔 Sacrifice without truth
