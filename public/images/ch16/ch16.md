## **Chapter 16 – The Love That Refused to Die (October–November 2025)**

Breakup ayyaka…
Silence vachindi.

But **love matram alaane undi**.



**One Week Later**

Breakup ayyi **one week** complete ayindi.

Suddenly…
Notification.

**Message from Priyanka.**

Madhu chusadu.
Reply ivvaledu.

**Priyanka:**

> *“Kanna…”*
> *“Nenu alanti ammayi kaadu.”*
> *“Please nannu nammu.”*
> *“Nenu good person.”*

Madhu phone lock chesadu.
Heart lo pain perigindi.
But reply ivvaledu.
<BREAK>


**Weeks Passing… Same Pattern**

**Every week… once.**

Priyanka message chestundi.
Madhu chustadu.
But silence.

* One week
* Two weeks
* Three weeks

Messages tone change avtundi.
Hope → Fear → Tears.

<BREAK>

**November 15, 2025 – The Breaking Point**

Ee roju…
**Everything changed.**

Priyanka messages continuous ga vastunnayi.

Madhu phone chetilo pattukoni…
Reply ivvaledu.
But **tears phone screen meeda padutunnayi.**



**Priyanka’s Long Message (Heart Completely Open)**

**Priyanka:**

> *“Kanna… please Kanna.”*
> *“Naa maatalu vinava?”*
> *“Nenu idhi cheptunnanu just because I want you.”*
> *“Nuvvu past lo em chesina naku parledhu.”*

Madhu tears wipe chesukuntadu.
Message scroll chestadu.

**Priyanka:**

> *“Nenu past lo em cheyyaledu Kanna.”*
> *“Please nammu.”*

Madhu hands shake avtunnayi.
Reply ledu.

<BREAK>

**Then… She Uses the Most Painful Word**

**Priyanka:**

> *“Bava…”*
> *“Nuvvu naa bava kada?”*
> *“Please bava, naatho matladu.”*
> *“Nenu em tappu cheyyaledu bava.”*
> *“Nenu ala kaadu bava.”*

Madhu eyes close ayyai.



**Complete Surrender of Love**

**Priyanka:**

> *“Nuvvu kavali bava.”*
> *“Nuvvu kavali ante nenu emaina chestha.”*
> *“Naku em vaddu.”*
> *“Nenu anni sacrifice chestha.”*
> *“Nuvvu maatram undu.”*

> *“Nuvvu entha bad ga behave chesina parledhu.”*
> *“Entha tittina parledhu.”*
> *“Please naa character ni doubt cheyyaku.”*

> *“Nuvvu naa first love bava.”*
> *“Nuvve naa last love kuda.”*
> *“Nenu evaritho tiragaledu.”*
> *“Nenu evaritho relationship lo ledu.”*

> *“Oka ammayi malli malli thirigi neekosam vasthundi ante…”*
> *“Once think cheyyi.”*
> *“Enta love undo artham avtundi.”*

<BREAK>

Madhu ee messages choodadam aapesaadu. Cell phone pakkan petti padukunnadu...

## **End of Chapter 16**

💔 Love that begged
💔 Love that surrendered
💔 Love that was still ignored
