## **Chapter 6 – The Truth That Hurt (2018)**

*The moment Madhu realised love also has rules… painful rules.*

2018 year.
Madhu ki appudu **18 years** daggara age.
Life lo oka important phase —
Madhu **own sister marriage matches** start ayyayi.

House lo peddalu, relatives, panulu…
matches choodadam, gothralu verify cheyyadam,
horoscopes, caste subdivision — anni rounds nadusthunnayi.

A time lo Madhu life ni completely marchipettese
**one truth** first time telisindi…
<BREAK>


**Modallu – Thirumandarallu**

Madhu caste lo okka **sub-caste system** undedi.
Two groups:

* **Modallu**
* **Thirumandarallu**

And rule enti ante…
*Thirumandarallu → must marry Thirumandarallu.*
*Modallu → must marry Modallu.*

Cross marriage okay kaadu…
not legally — but emotionally, culturally, mythically…
People strongly believe it.
<BREAK>
Madhu family lo andariki telisina rule idi.
Kaani Madhu ki matram **2018–2019** lo maatrame
clear ga strike ayyindi —
tana sister marriage match conversations valla.

Madhu himself is **Thirumandarallu**.
Tana sister kuda ade.

So matches search chesthunna peddalu
constant ga cheptu unnar:

**“Thirumandarallu vallatone match choodali…”**

Madhu silent ga vinnadu…
kani heart lo okka shock laaga feel ayyadu.



**Priyanka is Modallu.**

Aa moment lone
Madhu mind lo flash ayyindi
**only one face… Priyanka.**

Years nunchi feelings unde…
valalo silent understanding unde…
kani oka official dream kuda kaaledu.
He was too young earlier.

But ippudu…
rules vinagane
heart lo okka soft crack laaga kaligindi.

Madhu thought to himself:

**“Nenu Thirumandarallu…
Aame Modallu…
Ante… nenu eligible kaadhaa?”**

Status difference inka pain meedha sare…
Priyanka family koncham rich…
Madhu family simple middle.

Also **sub-caste difference**?
Adi ayithe break cheyyadaniki kastam,
especially village families lo.

Madhu kooda ardham chesukunnadu…
*Ee feeling ki future unda?
Ledu?*

<BREAK>

**He didn’t kill the feeling… he just locked it**

Madhu heart lo Priyanka gurinchi
oka soft love feeling already undedi.
Childhood nundi slow ga peri ayina affection.

Kaani ippudu
**he felt he cannot reach her.**
Not because she doesn’t like him…
but because **rules** don’t allow it.

So Madhu chesina only thing:

**He didn’t delete the feeling.
He didn’t express it.
He just placed it gently inside his heart…
and locked it in silence.**

Priyanka ki teliyadu…
Madhu matram aalochinchadu…

Ilaage Chapter 6 end ayipoyindi —
okka chinna truth,
Madhu heart lo pedda silent pain.
