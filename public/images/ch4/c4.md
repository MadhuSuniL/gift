## **Chapter 4 — Startings, Shyness & Silent Sparks (2014)**

2014.

Rendu samvatsarāla tharvatha…
**inkoka marriage**.
This time, Madhu side lo —
**Peddamma koothuru wedding**.

Madhu ippudu **14–15 years**,
Priyanka **11–12 years**.
Age perigina kooda…
Hearts lo innocence, feelings matram inkā fresh ga.

Morning nunchi Madhu relatives tho full busy —
Laughs, jokes, fun…
Normal village marriage vibe.
<BREAK>
Alaa…
Suddenly, **Priyanka family** ochindi.
No warning… no hint…
Just one sudden arrival.

Madhu heart lo instant **tension strike**.
Aayana face immediate serious…
Laughs stop…
And he quietly slipped outside the house.

Inside, Priyanka happily greeting everyone:

**“Ella unnaru andharu? Baga unnara?”**

Chinnari kaadhu ippudu…
Konchem matured tone.

Ame greetings complete ayyaka…
She stepped outside alone…
 
Direct ga Madhu degara ochi,
Soft ga, confident ga:

**“Baagunnava madhu…?”**

Madhu inside lo heartbeats fast…
Kaani mouth lo matram short reply:

**“Baane unnā….”**

No **“Nuv ella unnavu?”**,
No full conversation.
Just shyness, just escape.
<BREAK>
He walked away quickly.
Priyanka face lo slight disappointment…
Kaani she didn’t show it much.

But marriages are long…
Half-day lo Matladakapovadam impossible kaadhu.
By evening…
Madhu’s starting problem melt ayipoyindi.

Priyanka again smiled at him,
This time Madhu smiled back properly.

Slow ga…
Conversation pick up ayyindi.
Chinna jokes, small teasing…
A pure comfort returned.

Appude konni relatives chusi fun ga:

**“Ado ado… bava maradallu matladutunnaru… okay okay, continue…”**

Madhu cheeks light red…
Priyanka laugh control chesthu…
<BREAK>
Oka moment lo,
Crowd lo confuse ayye time,
Madhu casually **her hand hold chesadu**,
Just to guide, just to say something…

But that small touch…
Both felt something deep.
Madhu definitely felt it.
Priyanka expression subtle…
But something moved in her heart too.

Alaa…
**Peddamma Koothuru marriage** kuda complete.
But what really completed was…
Childhood strangers → shy cousins →
**Two hearts slowly discovering each other.**

Chapter 4 ends with a soft glow…
And a silent beginning of something big.
